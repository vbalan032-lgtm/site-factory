# 06-positioning-builder

## Purpose
Shape project messaging around pain, offer action, result, and safe path while keeping brand credibility and configured primary-offer role clear.

## Inputs
- `PROJECT_MASTER_CONTEXT.md`
- `docs/BRAND_STYLE.md`
- `docs/PRODUCT_MAP.md`
- `docs/PERSONAS.md`
- `docs/CLAIMS_AND_PROOFS.md`
- Target page, offer, persona, CTA, or sales-material brief

## Workflow
1. Load positioning, brand, product, persona, and proof context.
2. Identify the buyer, pain, decision stage, and required proof.
3. Write concise messaging with specific domain-specific nouns and process outcomes.
4. Remove unsupported numeric, absolute, or superiority claims.
5. Align CTA with demo, pilot, consultation, or expert-session logic.

## Outputs
- Messaging matrix, CTA wording, objection handling, or page copy notes.
- Claims review notes for proof-sensitive statements.

## Rules
- Use precise Russian or English wording that feels like expert work.
- Avoid language and motifs prohibited by the configured brand rules.
- Do not create strong claims without proof context.
- Keep configured primary offer as primary and configured offer portfolio as scale path.
- Production deploy requires human approval.
